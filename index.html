DonutLCR is a utility mod made for DonutSMP. It brings useful tools and market helpers together in one place, helping players track their balance, manage investments, spot Lag Crash Resell opportunities, and stay on top of item-price changes.

Whether you are checking your progress, watching the market, or waiting for the right time to buy and resell, DonutLCR is designed to make the DonutSMP experience more convenient.

BalTracker

Keep track of your in-game balance and monitor how your money changes over time. BalTracker helps you follow your progress without having to remember every transaction yourself.

Auto Invest

Investment tools designed to make managing your DonutSMP investments easier. Configure and use the feature carefully, and review your settings before relying on automated actions.

LCR — Lag Crash Resell

Tools for finding and responding to Lag Crash Resell opportunities. Keep an eye on market situations where item prices shift and a potential resell opportunity appears.

Elytra Price-Drop Alerts

Get warned when Elytra prices drop, so you can react to market changes without constantly checking prices yourself.

More DonutSMP Utilities

DonutLCR is built to bring useful DonutSMP quality-of-life features into one place, with room for more tools and improvements in future releases.

DonutLCR is intended for use with DonutSMP. Check the release download and its notes for the Minecraft version, mod loader, and any dependencies supported by that specific build.

What is DonutLCR?

DonutLCR is a DonutSMP utility mod featuring BalTracker, Auto Invest, LCR (Lag Crash Resell), Elytra price-drop alerts, and other helpful tools.

What does LCR mean?

LCR stands for Lag Crash Resell — watching for price changes and reselling opportunities in the DonutSMP market.

What does BalTracker do?

BalTracker helps you keep track of your in-game balance and your progress over time.

What does Auto Invest do?

Auto Invest provides investment-related convenience tools. Check the settings and release notes for the exact behavior available in your version.

Will DonutLCR warn me when Elytra prices drop?

Yes. Elytra price-drop alerts are one of the features described for DonutLCR.

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

Supported Minecraft versions and mod loaders depend on the release. Check the version details included with the file you download.

Where can I report a bug?

Use the project support or issue-reporting link provided by the DonutLCR maintainer, and include your Minecraft version, mod loader, DonutLCR version, and steps to reproduce the issue.
